# Recorded 1-D heat validation results

This directory records measured results from the OpenFOAM/PINN experiment performed with the settings in ../benchmarks/openfoam_heat1d/README.md. The detailed narrative report is intentionally not included yet; it will be added separately.

## Main result

The analytical solution is the reference for both numerical methods:

\[
u(x,t)=e^{-0.1\pi^2 t}\sin(\pi x).
\]

The refined OpenFOAM case (800 cells, time step 0.000125) reported a final-time relative L2 error of 6.595060e-05 and a final-time maximum absolute error of 2.458012e-05. Its flattened space-time relative L2 error across 800 cell centres and 101 saved times was 2.856959e-05.

The hard-constrained PINN with causal weighting reported a flattened space-time relative L2 error of 9.247381e-05 on that same evaluation grid. Without causal weighting, the corresponding error was 9.994022e-05.

These are single-seed measurements (seed 42), not multi-seed statistical estimates.

## Files

- summary.csv — aggregated measurements, with the evaluation grid and metric definition recorded in each row.
- selected_time_metrics.csv — the 11 selected times printed in the terminal reports, from t=0 through t=1 in steps of 0.1. This is not the full 101-snapshot output.
- figures/openfoam_refinement.svg — OpenFOAM error at selected times for the 200- and 800-cell cases.
- figures/common_grid_summary.svg — common-grid space-time relative L2 errors for the baseline PINN, both hard-constrained variants and refined OpenFOAM.
- figures/pinn_causal_ablation.svg — time-slice error curves for the hard-constrained PINN with and without causal weighting.

The full per-time CSVs generated by the comparison scripts are written locally under artifacts/ and are ignored by Git. They can be regenerated by running the benchmark instructions.

## Metric definitions and cautions

- Relative L2 at a time t is ||u_num(t)-u_exact(t)||_2 / ||u_exact(t)||_2 over the spatial cell centres.
- Flattened space-time relative L2 is the norm of the difference over all saved spatial/time samples divided by the norm of the exact field over those same samples.
- Mean, RMS, median and worst time-slice statistics are computed from the 101 per-time relative L2 values; unlike the flattened metric, they give each saved time slice equal weight.
- “PINN versus OpenFOAM normalized by exact” means ||u_PINN-u_OF||_2 / ||u_exact||_2, not normalization by the OpenFOAM field norm.
- The coarse/refined OpenFOAM comparison changed both cell count and time step, so it does not isolate spatial or temporal convergence order.
- The generalized example’s 200 x 200 error is reported separately because it was measured on a different evaluation grid.
- The initial OpenFOAM field was initialized from the analytical solution at the cell centres, so a near-zero t=0 field error is expected by construction.
- These results validate this one-dimensional diffusion benchmark only. They do not establish general CFD accuracy or statistical superiority of one PINN training method.
